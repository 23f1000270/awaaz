Awaaz आवाज़

Hears the ambulance before you do.

A small neural network listens for emergency sirens. It's the same model that runs on a ₹500 ESP32-S3 with two microphones, where it also lights an LED on the side the siren is coming from. Here it runs in this tab. No sound is recorded or uploaded.

Not listening

To try it for real, play an ambulance siren video on your phone near this device. The test siren is synthetic: a wail mixed into traffic noise, fed straight to the detector and played through your speakers.

How well it works

On 132 held-out siren recordings it raised the alarm for 88%, a median of 1.6 seconds into the clip. It raised no false alarms in 20 minutes of held-out road noise, though that is too little audio to give a reliable false-alarm rate. Mixed into other traffic noise as loud as the siren itself (0 dB), it still caught 89% of sirens. Synthetic wail, yelp and hi-lo sirens, which it never trained on, were caught 88 to 100% of the time. All of this comes from recordings and simulation. Road tests with the hardware come next.
